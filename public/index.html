How to stream audio (Crucial on Mac):

On macOS, browsers cannot capture audio from desktop apps (like native Spotify app). To stream music, play Spotify in a Chrome/Edge browser tab (open.spotify.com) and select that Chrome Tab with "Also share tab audio" checked!
