Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 • JamRoom Open Source Project

1. Zero-Login & Zero Personal Data Collection

JamRoom is built on a strict privacy-first foundation. We do not require account registration, passwords, email addresses, credit cards, or identity verification. We do not collect, harvest, profile, or sell your personal information.

2. No Audio Recording or Permanent Storage

All audio transmitted through JamRoom flows in real-time through temporary WebRTC media pipelines. Audio streams are never recorded, stored, transcribed, cached on disk, or analyzed. Once an audio packet is rendered in a participant's browser, it is instantly discarded from memory.

3. Ephemeral Rooms & Access Tokens

Rooms are generated on-demand using random numeric codes. When the host stops streaming or participants disconnect, the room and its associated cryptographic access tokens are completely dissolved. No room history, participant rosters, or playback records are retained.

4. Third-Party Infrastructure

JamRoom relies on minimal, trusted infrastructure providers to deliver real-time WebRTC media:

  • LiveKit Cloud: Operates the Selective Forwarding Unit (SFU) network that routes live WebRTC audio packets between participants. Media is forwarded in-memory without persistent storage.
  • Vercel: Hosts the static web client and token-minting serverless functions. Standard ephemeral edge logs (IP addresses and request timestamps) are retained in accordance with Vercel's standard security policies.

5. Cookies & Local Tracking

JamRoom does not use marketing cookies, tracking pixels, or third-party analytics trackers. Minimal ephemeral state (such as room codes or volume settings) may be temporarily held in your browser's active session memory and is never transmitted to marketing entities.

6. Open Source Transparency

JamRoom is completely open source under the MIT License. The entire source code is publicly auditable on GitHub to verify our privacy and security claims.
